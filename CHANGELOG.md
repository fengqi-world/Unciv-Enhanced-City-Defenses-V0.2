# Changelog

## V0.2
- Removed the Castle +5% Military Unit Production bonus.
- Walls: +3 City Strength, +100 City Health, +1 Production, 1 Gold/turn maintenance.
- Castle: +5 City Strength, +100 City Health, +2 Production, 2 Gold/turn maintenance.
- Arsenal: +7 City Strength, +100 City Health, +10% Military Unit Production, 3 Gold/turn maintenance.
- Military Base: +11 City Strength, +100 City Health, +10% Military Unit Production, 4 Gold/turn maintenance.
- Updated Walls of Babylon and Mughal Fort to receive their corresponding tier enhancements while retaining their original special effects.
- Added +200% Strength when defending against City attacks to all Siege-type units.
