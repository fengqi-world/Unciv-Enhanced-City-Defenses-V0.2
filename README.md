# Enhanced City Defenses

An Unciv extension mod that strengthens city-defense buildings and improves siege-unit survivability against city attacks.

## City-defense rules

| Building | City Strength | City Health | Production | Military Unit Production | Maintenance |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Walls | +3 | +100 | +1 | — | 1 Gold/turn |
| Castle | +5 | +100 | +2 | — | 2 Gold/turn |
| Arsenal | +7 | +100 | — | +10% | 3 Gold/turn |
| Military Base | +11 | +100 | — | +10% | 4 Gold/turn |

These values are cumulative through the defensive-building chain.

A city with Walls + Castle + Arsenal + Military Base therefore gains:

- +26 City Strength
- +400 City Health
- +3 Production
- +20% Production when constructing Military units
- 10 Gold/turn total maintenance

All original prerequisites, technologies, production costs and other unchanged effects remain as in the vanilla ruleset.

## Special defensive replacements

### Walls of Babylon
Keeps its vanilla special identity and receives the Walls-tier enhancement:

- +9 City Strength
- +200 City Health
- +1 Production
- 1 Gold/turn maintenance

### Mughal Fort
Keeps its vanilla special Gold effect and receives the Castle-tier enhancement:

- +12 City Strength
- +125 City Health
- +2 Production
- +5% Production when constructing Military units
- 2 Gold/turn maintenance

## Siege-unit rule

All units of the vanilla **Siege** unit type receive:

> **+200% Strength when defending against City attacks**

This only applies when the Siege unit is defending against a City. It does not increase its attack against cities and does not affect defense against ordinary military units.
